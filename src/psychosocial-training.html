What's included

Training built around how your workplace actually operates

Delivered face-to-face or virtually, and scaled to your site, your industry and your team.

01

For leaders & managers

Leaders learn to recognise the early signs of high job demands, low role clarity, poor support and workplace conflict, and how to respond before a pattern escalates into a complaint or a claim.

02

For frontline teams

Workers learn what a psychosocial hazard actually is, how to raise a concern, and what happens once they do, so raising an issue doesn't feel like a gamble.

03

Format & delivery

Sessions run face-to-face or virtually. We use real scenarios, not generic slides, so the content lands for construction crews, healthcare teams or office-based staff alike.

04

Legal duties, in plain language

We translate Australian health and safety laws, including your psychosocial hazard obligations and the hierarchy of control, into what it actually means for a manager's day-to-day decisions.

How we teach it

From regulator's desk to training room

We spent years explaining these obligations to employers as WorkSafe inspectors, so we know where the confusion usually sits and where the gaps between policy and practice actually show up.

Training is one control among several, and we're upfront about that. If training is the only thing in place, we'll tell you what else needs to happen alongside it, because that's what the Regulations require and it's what actually reduces risk.

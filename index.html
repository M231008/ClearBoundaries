Why organisations work with us

We know what good looks like from the regulator's side

Our founders are former WorkSafe psychosocial inspectors. We've conducted regulatory investigations, assessed employer compliance, and issued improvement notices. That experience means we know exactly what good looks like from the regulator's perspective, and we bring that knowledge to every engagement.

A lot of consultancies will help you document your risks and tick your compliance boxes. We do that too, but it's not where we stop. We work with organisations to shift how people are led, supported, and treated at work.

Founders

Two frontlines.
The same pattern.

Clear Boundaries brings together frontline experience, regulatory insight and an understanding of what psychosocial harm looks like in real workplaces.

Abbie Janssen

Co-founder · Former WorkSafe Inspector

Abbie started her career as a paramedic. Then a back injury took that career away from her, and gave her something else instead: a firsthand understanding of what happens when workplaces don't look after their people.

She moved into occupational health and safety, completed a postgraduate qualification in OHS and Environmental Management Systems, and spent years as a WorkSafe inspector, walking into businesses after bullying, harassment, or unmanageable workload had already taken its toll, and seeing exactly what the good workplaces did differently.

Former paramedicPostgrad OHS & EMSFormer WorkSafe inspector

Charlie Cole

Co-founder · Former WorkSafe Psychosocial Inspector

Charlie's path ran alongside a different version of the same problem. A police officer with both New Zealand and Victoria Police, then a WorkSafe Victoria psychosocial inspector, her background is in gathering evidence, interviewing people, and working out what happened and why.

Being Māori and Pākehā, she's always brought a cross-cultural lens to that work, and she knows what it's like to be on the other side of it too, having experienced bullying, harassment, and job demands that never let up.

Former NZ & VIC PoliceFormer WorkSafe psychosocial inspectorCross-cultural practice

Two different frontlines. The same pattern, over and over: organisations reacting to psychosocial hazards only after the damage was already done. That's why Clear Boundaries exists, not to add more paperwork, but to help organisations get ahead of the risk.
